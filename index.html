How do I schedule a ride?

Call us or send a request using the form below. We’ll review the details and contact you. Sending a form does not reserve a ride; our team will confirm your arrangements.

Can you accommodate a wheelchair?

Wheelchair transportation inquiries are welcome. Please tell us about your chair and assistance needs so we can confirm a suitable option before scheduling.

Do you travel outside Lubbock?

Yes, we welcome inquiries for Lubbock and surrounding areas, including trips up to 120 miles. Contact us to confirm your route and availability.

What does a ride cost?

Contact us for a quote based on your route and transportation needs. We’ll discuss pricing before you confirm your ride.

Can facilities arrange transportation?

Yes. Individuals, families, and facilities are welcome to contact us about one-time rides, recurring transportation, and facility contracts.

Is this an emergency transportation service?

No. Our transportation is for planned, non-emergency trips. For a medical emergency, call 911.
