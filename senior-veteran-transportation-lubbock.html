Senior & veteran transportation in Lubbock

Staying connected means being able to reach the people and places that matter. Cotton Country Transport serves Lubbock and surrounding areas and welcomes ride inquiries from seniors, veterans, family members, and caregivers.

Arrange a ride for yourself or someone else

Tell us who will be traveling and how to reach the person coordinating the trip. Share the pickup and destination areas, preferred timing, and any transportation assistance needed. A family member can start the conversation on a loved one’s behalf.

Discuss companions and recurring trips

Ask about a companion traveling with the rider or about recurring trips for appointments and activities. Space, scheduling, and assistance depend on the trip and must be confirmed. Any insurance, benefit, or third-party payment arrangement must be discussed directly; coverage is not assumed.

What to have ready

  • Pickup and destination cities or areas
  • Preferred travel date and appointment or arrival time
  • Whether you need one-way or return transportation
  • Mobility assistance, companion, and vehicle requirements
  • A phone number for the person coordinating the trip

Pricing and availability

Contact us for a quote based on your route and transportation needs. The team will discuss pricing and availability before you confirm. Online inquiries do not reserve a ride. For a time-sensitive trip, please call.
