Medical transportation in Lubbock

Getting to a scheduled appointment should be part of your care plan, not an unanswered question. Cotton Country Transport welcomes inquiries for planned medical transportation in Lubbock and the surrounding South Plains.

Request a ride ↗Call (806) 701-0404

Plan around your appointment

Share your appointment time as well as your preferred pickup time. Include the city or area for both ends of the trip. Let us know whether you need a one-way ride or would like to discuss return transportation; return arrangements must be confirmed separately.

Tell us about assistance needs

If walking, transfers, or a wheelchair affect your transportation needs, discuss those details before scheduling. Our team must confirm a suitable option for your trip. This is planned transportation, not an ambulance or a source of medical care.

What to have ready

  • Pickup and destination cities or areas
  • Preferred travel date and appointment or arrival time
  • Whether you need one-way or return transportation
  • Mobility assistance, companion, and vehicle requirements
  • A phone number for the person coordinating the trip

Pricing and availability

Contact us for a quote based on your route and transportation needs. The team will discuss pricing and availability before you confirm. Online inquiries do not reserve a ride. For a time-sensitive trip, please call.
