Start with the transportation details

Hospitals, rehabilitation centers, care facilities, and other organizations are welcome to contact Cotton Country Transport about planned rides in Lubbock and surrounding communities.

For an initial inquiry, share the pickup and destination areas, proposed date, passenger mobility needs, and a coordinator’s contact details. Do not send medical records or confidential patient information through the public website.

One-time rides and recurring needs

Ask about individual transportation requests, recurring schedules, and facility contracts. Availability, vehicle suitability, pricing, and any account arrangements are discussed directly. No contract or recurring schedule is established by submitting a form.

Confirm arrangements directly

For discharge transportation or a time-sensitive request, call (806) 701-0404. Confirm timing, assistance, and receiving arrangements with our team before relying on a ride. This is non-emergency transportation.
