Dialysis transportation in Lubbock

Repeated appointments take planning. Cotton Country Transport welcomes inquiries about rides to dialysis appointments in Lubbock and nearby communities, including transportation needs that follow a recurring schedule.

Request a ride ↗Call (806) 701-0404

Discuss your recurring schedule

Tell us which days you travel, your appointment times, and the pickup and destination areas. Include your desired start date. We will discuss availability before making arrangements; sending an inquiry does not establish a standing reservation.

Plan the return trip

Appointment duration and departure times may vary. Discuss your expected return timing and any mobility assistance before confirming a ride. Ask how to contact the team when your appointment schedule changes so the transportation plan can be reviewed.

What to have ready

  • Pickup and destination cities or areas
  • Preferred travel date and appointment or arrival time
  • Whether you need one-way or return transportation
  • Mobility assistance, companion, and vehicle requirements
  • A phone number for the person coordinating the trip

Pricing and availability

Contact us for a quote based on your route and transportation needs. The team will discuss pricing and availability before you confirm. Online inquiries do not reserve a ride. For a time-sensitive trip, please call.
