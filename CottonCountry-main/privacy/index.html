YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Updated October 5, 2026

This notice describes information submitted through the Cotton Country Transportation website.

What you share

The inquiry form collects your name, contact details, trip preferences, and the message you choose to provide. Please do not submit medical records, payment details, or sensitive health information.

How it is used

Information you submit is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss transportation arrangements. Form submissions are not confirmed ride reservations.

Website and form providers

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. When the online form is enabled, Formspree processes submissions on our behalf. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting, security, and service operation. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Formspree privacy policy · GitHub privacy statement

Questions about your information

To ask about information you submitted or request its deletion, contact info@cottoncountrytransport.com or call (806) 701-0404 .

Return to the website

Optional analytics

Analytics are disabled by default. If enabled, this website asks for your permission before loading Google Analytics. With permission, it measures page visits, clicks on call links, and successful form submissions. A phone-link click does not confirm a completed call.

Our custom events do not include form contents, names, email addresses, passenger details, or phone numbers. Page URLs sent to analytics omit query strings and fragments. Google may process technical information and use cookies after you opt in. You can change your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer when analytics is enabled.
