Transportation inquiries

This website provides information and a way to contact Cotton Country Transportation. Submitting a form does not confirm a reservation, price, pickup time, or recurring schedule. Our team must confirm arrangements directly.

Availability and suitability

Transportation depends on route, scheduling, vehicle suitability, and the assistance needed. Discuss your requirements and receive confirmation before relying on a ride.

Non-emergency service

The service is for planned, non-emergency transportation. It is not an ambulance or a substitute for medical care. For a medical emergency, call 911.

Quotes and trip policies

Confirm pricing, payment, cancellation arrangements, and other trip-specific terms with the team before booking. This website does not establish a separate rate or cancellation policy.

Your information

Please provide accurate contact and trip details. Do not submit medical records, payment card details, or sensitive health information through the public forms. See our privacy notice.
